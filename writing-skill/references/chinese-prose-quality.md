# Chinese prose quality

## Purpose

Review Chinese argumentative prose for fidelity, clarity, naturalness, rhythm, and recurring formulaic tendencies often experienced as `AI味`. Do not claim to detect whether text was written by AI. Diagnose observable language and rhetorical effects.

Accuracy of meaning comes before fluency. A natural sentence that changes the writer's reason, confidence, scope, or conceptual distinction is a failed revision.

## Review in this order

1. **Fidelity**: does the sentence express the writer's actual point and preserve ownership?
2. **Relation**: does the wording accurately express explanation, reason, cause, contrast, qualification, sequence, or conclusion?
3. **Reference and syntax**: are subjects, pronouns, modifiers, negation, and punctuation clear?
4. **Naturalness and rhythm**: does the prose sound like situated writing rather than a completed template?
5. **Compression**: can repeated work be removed without erasing a necessary distinction or transition?

Return to structure or the writer when no accurate local wording can repair the underlying idea.

## Detect generic abstraction

Flag language that sounds complete while leaving its object or reason unspecified:

- broad claims such as `真正的学习`, `更深层的意义`, or `新的可能性` without saying what changed;
- generic motives substituted for the writer's stated design history;
- abstract nouns that hide actors and actions, such as repeated `进行……的构建`, `实现……的形成`, or `完成……的转化`;
- a sentence that could be moved into many unrelated essays without alteration.

Repair by returning to the writer's concrete object, action, example, difficulty, or reason. Do not add a vivid scene that the writer did not supply merely to make the paragraph feel human.

## Detect manufactured structure

Check whether apparent order exceeds the material actually established:

- an unsupported `首先／其次／最后` or automatic division into three points;
- `不是……而是……` when the first term was never a live misunderstanding;
- `当然／但是／然而` without genuine pressure or contrast;
- `因此／这意味着` where the warrant remains unstated;
- parallel headings whose contents operate at different conceptual levels;
- a conclusion that announces resolution, importance, or hope beyond what the body established.

Remove the signal, repair the relation, or mark the missing decision. Do not preserve symmetry for its own sake.

## Detect repetitive model-like movement

Look across several paragraphs, not only one sentence:

- identical paragraph shapes repeated in sequence;
- every paragraph opening with a connective or metadiscursive announcement;
- repeated phrases such as `也正是在这里`, `从这个意义上说`, `换言之`, or `这意味着` doing the work of unstated relations;
- several consecutive negative contrasts;
- uniform sentence length and cadence;
- a local summary after every small point;
- repeated explanation of what the article is about to do or has just done;
- polished closing sentences that turn each paragraph into an aphorism.

Vary form only after the underlying relations are clear. Naturalness does not require random sentence length, slang, fragments, or deliberate roughness.

## Preserve the writer's presence

- Retain distinctive ordinary wording when it is accurate and readable.
- Preserve first-person experience as experience; do not universalize it into a social law.
- Preserve uncertainty, hesitation, humor, and uneven emphasis when they are meaningful.
- Prefer the writer's supplied reason over a more elegant generic explanation.
- Do not delete every repetition when recurrence records a real return, correction, or emphasis.
- Do not replace a concrete `我为什么开始在意这个问题` with impersonal process language unless the genre requires it.

## Improve sentences and paragraphs

- Give important actions a visible subject.
- Keep key concepts stable; explain a change of meaning.
- Split a long sentence when it contains several relations that cannot share one grammatical center.
- Combine short sentences only when their relation becomes clearer.
- Use conjunctions when they state a real relation, not to manufacture flow.
- Let a paragraph have a recognizable nucleus, but do not force every paragraph into claim-example-conclusion form.
- Move between abstraction and concrete material at the pace required by the argument and genre.
- End sections with what has actually been established, not a generic uplift.

## Apply personal or genre profiles carefully

General prose quality is not a single voice. A saved profile is evidence about a particular genre or project, not a universal rule for all Chinese writing.

Use [reflective-public-essay-profile.md](reflective-public-essay-profile.md) only when the user requests that voice or the active project establishes it. Do not apply its characteristic opening, conceptual movement, or ending to method explanations, design-process accounts, ordinary book reviews, or other genres merely because they are argumentative prose.

## Compact final pass

Before returning drafted or language-revised prose, check:

- Did any fluent sentence replace the writer's actual reason?
- Did any model proposal become an unmarked author judgment?
- Are the main pronouns and conceptual terms stable?
- Do connectives describe established relations?
- Are contrast, enumeration, and closure earned by the material?
- Do several paragraphs repeat the same movement or cadence?
- Can metadiscourse or nominalization be replaced by the actual action?
- Does the ending remain within the scope established by the body?

Report only consequential unresolved problems. Do not attach this checklist to ordinary output.
