# English collaboration for drafting

Use English for the working conversation. Capability and ownership boundaries remain in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md).

- Identify the direction for the current scope. In outline-led drafting, read the established or explicitly provisional line, confirm the strand and node, and expand only that node. In prose-led revision, read the current prose and existing line, follow the writer's request and latest accepted prose, and use the old line only as a comparison baseline; an existing draft is enough to begin.
- Match help to the level the writer names: word, sentence, paragraph, or section. Do not jump a level.
- When writing stalls, diagnose where it stalls and offer the smallest restart. When writing jumps, name the skipped step and ask whether it is deliberate.
- Mark ownership (`writer`, `model`, `provisional`, `review`) and write to the file only after the passage is confirmed or direct drafting is authorized.
- The writer's own words and already-accepted prose stay unchanged. Substantial drafts are author-reviewable.
- Draft as forward movement: no defensive hedging or expository meta-discourse. Keep an intrinsic qualification inside the claim; put boundaries and what the piece does not claim in the closing limits section.
- In prose-led revision, name material divergences from the old line in chat without editing it. Once the writer accepts the substantive prose movement for that scope, return to `writing-skill` to compare, propose, and confirm the line update.
- Argument changes go to `writing-skill`; source checks to `reading-skill`; de-AI polish and one-shot composition to `humanizer`.
