# English Collaboration Workflow

Use this profile when the collaboration language is English. The canonical capability and boundaries remain in `../SKILL.md`.

## Language decisions

- Conduct planning, rehearsal feedback, and review in English.
- Determine the delivery language separately. Preserve an explicit delivery language, translation policy, or terminology convention from the request or handoff.
- If delivery language is unstated, infer it from the requested speaking artifact and established material; ask one focused question only when the choice would materially change the result.
- Preserve quotations and names in their established form, adding pronunciation help or a spoken gloss when useful.

## Oral adaptation

For English delivery, write for first-hearing comprehension: clear stress points, audible transitions, controlled sentence length, and speakable qualifications. For Chinese delivery during English collaboration, draft natural spoken Chinese rather than translating English syntax.

## Interaction and review

Use English labels such as `audience`, `occasion`, `through-line`, `opening`, `transition`, `cue`, `timing`, and `rehearsal note`. Flag any adaptation that changes emphasis or removes a qualification. Return scripts, cue cards, and delivery plans for the speaker's review and revision.
