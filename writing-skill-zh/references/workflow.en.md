# English collaboration for Chinese writing

Use English for planning, questions, review comments, and change notes. The capability and author-review requirements remain in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md).

- This skill produces or edits Chinese prose. The language of discussion or sources does not change that target. Preserve quotations in supplied wording and label a requested model translation.
- Compose idiomatic Chinese rather than transferring English syntax or punctuation rules. Follow the established register, terminology, and authorial voice.
- Explain consequential choices about meaning, structure, evidence, uncertainty, or voice. Do not turn a local request into a systematic audit.
- Return outlines, drafts, continuations, and substantial revisions for author review. Surface consequential model proposals without requiring meaningless textual changes.
