# Chinese project writing

Read for a substantial Chinese argumentative article or section stored in a project, a requested persistent argument context, structural diagnosis, or a formal revision cycle. Use only the relevant parts. The ordinary workflow and Chinese language guidance remain in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md).

## Maintain the current 论证线

Before continuous prose for a substantial saved article or section, create or update a separate Markdown argument file beside the draft. Preserve a project's naming convention; otherwise use `论证线.md`. Also maintain one for a requested living outline or persistent structural state. A short paragraph, local language edit, or standalone review does not require one.

Record current argumentative intent:

- review status;
- governing question or rhetorical action and writer-owned central judgment, including its certainty and scope;
- core concepts and their meanings in this piece;
- intended reader's starting position, writer-reader relationship, intended movement, and voice evidence;
- claim path with explicit relations such as `解释`, `支持`, `限定`, or `对照`;
- each passage's job, ordered material, and assigned examples or established sources;
- consequential model proposals, writer decisions, source checks, and boundaries still open.

Use the simplest useful outline or table. A series of topics or questions does not by itself establish inference. Do not make the file a prose synopsis, source dump, paragraph script, project log, or task tracker. Preserve existing project layout and meaningful draft variants.

## Authority and author review

The writer's current correction outranks the file. Keep author judgments, established source claims, local composition decisions, substantive model proposals, and unresolved checks distinguishable.

Every Codex creation or modification resets the file's visible status to `待审阅`, including an edit that follows explicit writer comments or clarifies an existing point. Only the writer may set it to `已审阅`. Do not retain or restore a reviewed marker on their behalf. The current artifact awaits review even when its stable portions need no new substantive decision.

Before substantial drafting or revision, read the current argument file, affected prose, and latest instructions. When a central judgment, concept, relation, section job, evidence selection, or boundary changes, update the file before propagating that change through prose. Hold consequential model proposals for acceptance unless immediate drafting or outline-and-prose together is explicitly requested. Such authorization permits reviewable prose, not an unmarked settled author position. Local punctuation and wording changes that preserve the argument require no ceremonial outline update.

## Elaborate the outline and write for the reader

Keep supplied headings, judgments, reasons, examples, qualifications, and meaningful order. Add only the passage job, workable sequence of supplied material, relation to adjacent passages, and any unresolved choice. Do not replace a specific author reason with a familiar generic rationale or invent a new premise to make the outline look complete.

For a full article or substantial unsettled movement, return the detailed outline and stop before prose unless immediate drafting, outline-and-prose together, or an equivalent confirmed structure permits proceeding. A commentary plan is not an author-reviewable outline. A consequential unresolved author choice remains open when choosing it would make the prose misleading.

Derive an internal reader path before composing: where the reader enters, why this material matters now, what becomes clearer or more difficult, and where the passage leaves them. Do not create another required artifact. An opening question may invite identification without becoming the central question promised by the article. A boundary in the argument file is not automatically a disclaimer in the prose. Let central distinctions and necessary bridges receive space; use representative examples rather than compressing an entire inventory.

## Reconstruct and review the actual article

Read the prose independently of the intended outline. Ask:

1. What does the opening lead this reader to expect?
2. What central judgment or competing judgments does the body develop?
3. What work does each section perform, and why does the next follow?
4. What does the ending actually establish, and where does it place writer and reader?

Compare intended and realized paths without assuming that the old arrangement must be better. Repair accidental deviations in prose. Return a useful substantive new path as a proposal instead of silently rewriting the argument file to validate it afterward.

For argument audit, separate claim, grounds, warrant, backing, qualifier, and rebuttal where relevant. Explain how an example bears on a concept. Personal experience does not automatically establish a social pattern; an explanation does not settle an evaluation. Check mechanisms in causal claims and evaluative premises in normative conclusions. Test the strongest objection created by the actual scope, without requiring a separate counterargument section.

For a difficult connection, remove `因此／但是／当然` temporarily and state the relation. Distinguish cause, reason, sequence, evidence, qualification, concession, and a change of question or analytic level. Restore a missing middle step only when the material licenses it; otherwise flag the gap. Do not demand proof for a reader-facing invitation that asserts no premise, or let a repeated topic word stand in for an argument.

Prioritize a displaced governing question, unsupported claim, or structural gap over sentence polish. Review returns located evidence, effects, and conditional repair directions. The writer chooses among incompatible central positions.

## Formal revision when requested

Use a formal cycle only for requested systematic work, explicitly multi-round revision, or an established project convention. Ordinary edits do not need version labels, classification, a ledger, or scores.

Keep one authorized layer active: `整体结构` for argument and audience, `篇章关系` for neighboring units, or `语言` for expression and punctuation. A structural round includes necessary dependent wording, while unrelated polish waits. A language round preserves the central judgment, evidence roles, and claim path. An observation at another layer does not authorize changing it.

Follow the project's names and numbering. Keep deferred findings only when they must survive into later saved versions; distinguish structural and language issues from `需核对来源` and `待作者决定`. Reclassify or close them in the relevant round. After a round, state whether to remain, move to a local layer, return to structure, request an exact source check, return a substantive decision, or finish the current revision task. A completed language pass does not make the article author-approved.

After substantial prose, reconstruct the argument and reader movement, then check Chinese expression and fidelity. Apply later writer corrections across all affected passages. Report only consequential differences and unresolved decisions.
