# English Collaboration Workflow

Use this profile only after the writer explicitly invokes the Skill. The canonical capability and boundaries remain in `../SKILL.md`.

## Language decisions

- Conduct reflective dialogue in English unless the writer explicitly requests another collaboration language.
- Preserve the language of existing records, quotations, and established technical terms.
- When a record is requested, use [meta-reflection-record.en.md](meta-reflection-record.en.md) for English or [meta-reflection-record.zh-CN.md](meta-reflection-record.zh-CN.md) for Simplified Chinese.

## Reflective dialogue

Begin from the stage boundary and experience the writer names. Ask one focused question at a time and follow the writer's live center rather than completing every reflection lens. Distinguish writer statements, artifact evidence, unresolved uncertainty, and model observations.

## Record and review

Use first person only for wording supplied or explicitly accepted by the writer. Keep model reconstructions and suggestions in a clearly provisional section. Return the record for acceptance, revision, or rejection; acceptance without textual changes is sufficient confirmation.
