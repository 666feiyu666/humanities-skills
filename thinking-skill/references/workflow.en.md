# English Collaboration Workflow

Use this profile when the collaboration language is English. The canonical capability and boundaries remain in `../SKILL.md`.

## Language decisions

- Conduct Socratic dialogue, map discussion, reconstruction, and review in English.
- Preserve an established thinking-record, map, or writing-brief language unless the writer overrides it. For a new artifact, follow an explicit language request; otherwise use English.
- Notes, experiences, and imported maps do not determine the output language. Preserve quotations and established terms; label model translations.

## Thinking dialogue

Begin with the writer's live concern or the structure they supplied. Ask one focused question at a time, explain the distinction it is testing when useful, and let the answer redirect the inquiry. Use concise working labels such as `writer statement`, `model reconstruction`, `candidate connection`, `tension`, `open question`, and `decision pending`.

## Artifacts and review

Write maps and records in idiomatic English rather than translating a fixed Chinese structure. Keep writer-owned claims separate from model proposals. Treat a writing brief's language as an explicit handoff property when the writer has chosen it; do not infer it merely from the thinking record. Return all substantive artifacts for author review.
